THE LEDGER OF SINS

Chapter 5: The Long Way Back

"The Long Way Back" picks up right where the last chapter left off, and cassian's pacing doesn't waste the momentum — the psychological elements sharpen here, and a choice gets made that the rest of the story will keep circling back to.

It's a quieter chapter on the surface: a conversation, a decision, a door left open instead of closed. But for a story about the ledger of sins, quiet is usually where the real damage happens.

By the final page, the stakes have shifted just enough that going back feels impossible. Whatever comes next, it isn't going to be what anyone at the start of this chapter expected.
